Sunshine Coast Bike Racks

This map shows existing and desired public bike rack locations across the Sunshine Coast, BC, including Gibsons, Sechelt, and Roberts Creek.

Existing bike racks are sourced from OpenStreetMap , a free, community-maintained map.

If you notice a bike rack that is missing or incorrect, you can help by adding or updating it directly in OpenStreetMap.
